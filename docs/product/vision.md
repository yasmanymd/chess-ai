## Vision Statement

For casual chess players, learners, coaches, and students who want to play and learn together online, the AI-Assisted Chess Platform is a multilingual web platform that combines reliable real-time chess with a future educational environment.

Unlike a game-only prototype, the platform will establish a trustworthy chess domain and documented engineering foundation that can evolve toward lessons, exercises, coaching, game analysis, and competitions.

The project itself is also a public learning product: it shows how a human decision owner and AI implementation agents can design, build, verify, and explain a real software system.

## User Groups

- Casual players seeking simple online games.
- Learners who want to improve their chess skills.
- Coaches working with students.
- Competitive players who may later participate in tournaments.
- Software practitioners studying architecture and AI-assisted development.

## Long-Term Capabilities

- Online multiplayer chess.
- Persistent game history and replay.
- PGN interoperability.
- Courses and guided lessons.
- Tactical and strategic exercises.
- Move explanations and game analysis.
- Coach-and-student workflows.
- Opening exploration.
- Rankings, matchmaking, competitions, and tournaments.

Long-term capabilities are directional and require prioritization before implementation.
