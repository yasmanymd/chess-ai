## MVP Outcome

Deliver a publicly accessible, multilingual web application in which two people can create or join a chess game, play under server-validated rules, recover from a browser reload or temporary disconnection, persist the result, and review or exchange the game afterward.

## In Scope

- Temporary player identities.
- Game creation and joining.
- Two-player online chess.
- Server-authoritative rule validation and game state.
- Real-time synchronization.
- Reconnection and state recovery.
- Complete legal chess games.
- Persistent game result and movement history.
- Game history and move-by-move replay.
- PGN import and export.
- English, Spanish, and French user interfaces.
- Public deployment.
- Public setup, architecture, process, and verification documentation.

## Out of Scope

- Registered accounts and account administration.
- Elo ratings and rankings.
- Tournaments.
- Automatic matchmaking.
- Complete educational course management.
- Computer opponents.
- Advanced engine analysis such as Stockfish integration.
- Native mobile applications.
- Monetization.

## Scope Change Rule

An item moving into or out of MVP scope must include:

1. The reason for the change.
2. Its impact on milestones, architecture, risk, and verification.
3. The alternatives considered.
4. Explicit approval from Yasmany.
